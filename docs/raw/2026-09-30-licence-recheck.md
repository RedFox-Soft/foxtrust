# Licence re-check: Spamhaus DROP and abuse.ch (2026-09-30)

Checked live on 2026-09-30 while preparing licence confirmation requests.

## Spamhaus DROP

- https://www.spamhaus.org/drop/terms/ now redirects to
  https://www.spamhaus.org/blocklists/drop-fair-use-policy/ ("The Spamhaus Don't Route Or Peer
  Lists - Fair Use Policy").
- §2.1: "The DROP Lists are provided to you free of charge subject to these Terms."
- §3.1: "The content of the DROP List is protected by copyright and database right. Nothing in these
  Terms shall be construed as granting an assignment or licence of any intellectual property
  rights in the DROP Lists."
- §3.2: "YOU MAY UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES USE OR PERMIT ANY THIRD PARTY TO USE THE 'SPAMHAUS' NAME
  (INCLUDING ANY VARIATIONS, ALLUSIONS OR ITERATIONS OF THE 'SPAMHAUS' NAME OR ANY REFERENCE TO THE
  'SPAMHAUS DATA') IN YOUR MARKETING, PROMOTIONAL OR ANY OTHER COMMERCIAL MATERIALS."
- §3.3: "We reserve the right in our absolute discretion to revoke your right to use the DROP Lists
  for any reason".
- The list page https://www.spamhaus.org/blocklists/do-not-route-or-peer/ still says the data
  "should be available at no cost, regardless of size or business type, to protect internet users".
  The 2026-09-24 statement "when used in a product, credit must be given to Spamhaus Project" was
  not found on either page.
