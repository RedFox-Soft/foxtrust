# Privacy

## Your account

You sign in to FoxTrust with a foxauth account. FoxTrust stores only what it needs to run your keys:

- the identity foxauth gives us for you (the issuer and an opaque subject id), and when you last signed in;
- your API keys, as hashes only, with their labels and limits;
- how many lookups each key made per day, by outcome.

**Your name and email stay in foxauth.** FoxTrust shows them while you are signed in and keeps neither. To change them, or to delete them and your foxauth account, use your foxauth account settings.

Before you delete your foxauth account, revoke your API keys on your account page. FoxTrust is not told when a foxauth account is deleted, so keys you leave keep working until they are revoked; what remains of the account holds no name or email.

## Your lookups

FoxTrust does not store or log the addresses you look up. It keeps only per-key daily counts.

## Visitors of this site

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no scripts. A session cookie exists only while you are signed in. FoxTrust does not log page views with your address.
